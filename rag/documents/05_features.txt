# TechVex Solutions — Product Features & Specifications

## TechVex CRM Pro — Detailed Features

### Contact & Lead Management
- Unlimited contact records (Growth plan and above)
- 360-degree customer view (all interactions, emails, calls, tasks)
- Lead capture from website forms, WhatsApp, email, and social media
- Duplicate detection and automatic merging
- Custom fields (up to 50 per entity)
- Tags and segmentation

### AI-Powered Sales Features
- AI lead scoring (0-100) based on engagement, firmographics, and behavior
- Predictive deal closure probability
- Next best action recommendations
- Automated lead assignment based on territory and workload
- Smart follow-up reminders

### Pipeline Management
- Multiple sales pipelines
- Drag-and-drop deal management
- Stage-based probability weighting
- Deal aging alerts
- Revenue forecasting

### Communication Tools
- Two-way email sync (Gmail, Outlook, custom SMTP)
- WhatsApp Business integration
- SMS integration (Twilio, MSG91, Exotel)
- Call logging with VoIP integration
- Meeting scheduling with calendar sync (Google, Outlook)
- Email templates and sequences

### Automation
- Visual workflow builder
- Trigger-based automation (time, event, field change)
- Auto-assign leads
- Automated email sequences
- SLA tracking and escalation

### Reporting & Analytics
- 50+ pre-built reports
- Custom report builder
- Real-time dashboards
- Sales leaderboard
- Activity tracking
- Revenue analytics by product, region, and salesperson

### Mobile App
- iOS and Android native apps
- Offline mode for recent records
- Business card scanner
- Voice notes
- GPS check-in for field sales

## TechVex ERP Suite — Technical Specifications

### Financial Module
- Multi-company, multi-branch accounting
- GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9) automated
- TDS and TCS computation
- Bank reconciliation
- Multi-currency with real-time exchange rates
- P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow statements
- Budget vs Actual tracking

### HR & Payroll
- Employee lifecycle management
- Attendance (biometric, app, manual)
- Leave management (all statutory leave types)
- Payroll with PF, ESIC, PT, TDS computation
- Form 16 and payslip generation
- Performance management and appraisal

### Inventory Management
- Multi-warehouse management
- Batch and serial number tracking
- FIFO/LIFO/Weighted Average costing
- Minimum stock alerts
- Barcode/QR code generation
- Material Requirements Planning (MRP)

### Technology Stack
- Backend: Java / Node.js microservices
- Database: PostgreSQL with read replicas
- Frontend: React.js Progressive Web App
- Hosting: AWS India region (Mumbai)
- Security: AES-256 encryption, MFA, role-based access
- Uptime: 99.9% SLA

## System Requirements (Cloud)
- Browser: Chrome 80+, Firefox 75+, Edge 80+, Safari 14+
- Internet: 5 Mbps minimum (20 Mbps recommended for video features)
- Mobile: iOS 13+ or Android 9+

## System Requirements (On-Premise ERP)
- Server OS: Windows Server 2019+ or Ubuntu 20.04+
- RAM: 32 GB minimum (64 GB recommended)
- CPU: 8 cores minimum
- Storage: 500 GB SSD minimum
- Database: PostgreSQL 14+
