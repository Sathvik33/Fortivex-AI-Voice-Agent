# TechVex Solutions — Company Profile

## About TechVex Solutions

TechVex Solutions is a leading enterprise software company founded in 2015, headquartered in Bangalore, India with offices in Mumbai, Delhi, and Hyderabad.

We specialize in AI-powered enterprise software including CRM, ERP, Analytics, and Cloud Infrastructure solutions that help businesses streamline operations, improve customer relationships, and drive sustainable growth.

**Mission:** To empower businesses with intelligent software that automates complexity and amplifies human potential.

**Vision:** To be India's most trusted enterprise software partner by 2027.
