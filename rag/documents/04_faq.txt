# TechVex Solutions — Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

**Q: What is TechVex Solutions?**
A: TechVex Solutions is an Indian enterprise software company that provides AI-powered CRM, ERP, Analytics, and Cloud solutions. We serve 2,500+ clients across India and 15 countries.

**Q: How is TechVex different from other CRM providers like Salesforce or HubSpot?**
A: TechVex is built specifically for Indian businesses with GST compliance, regional language support, WhatsApp integration, Tally integration, and India-specific business workflows. Our pricing is also significantly more affordable than global platforms, with dedicated 24/7 support in Indian languages.

**Q: Do you offer a free trial?**
A: Yes! CRM Pro offers a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. Analytics Platform offers a 30-day trial. Contact us for an ERP demo.

**Q: Is my data safe with TechVex?**
A: Absolutely. We are ISO 27001 certified, SOC 2 Type II compliant, and follow GDPR standards. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Data is stored in Indian data centers.

## Implementation Questions

**Q: How long does implementation take?**
A: CRM Pro implementation takes 1-2 weeks. ERP Suite typically takes 2-6 months depending on company size. We offer an Express Implementation option (< 30 days) for urgency.

**Q: Do you provide training?**
A: Yes. All plans include free online training via our learning portal (100+ video tutorials). We also offer on-site training workshops at ₹25,000/day.

**Q: Can I migrate data from my current system?**

## Technical Questions

**Q: Can TechVex CRM integrate with WhatsApp?**
A: Yes. TechVex CRM Pro (Growth plan and above) includes official WhatsApp Business API integration for sending messages, tracking conversations, and automating follow-ups.

**Q: Does TechVex work offline?**
A: TechVex is a cloud-based solution and requires internet connectivity. The mobile app caches recently viewed records for offline reading, but editing requires connectivity.

**Q: What APIs do you provide?**
A: We offer a comprehensive REST API and webhooks for all plans (Professional and above). Our API documentation is available at docs.techvex.com.

**Q: Does TechVex support multiple currencies?**
A: Yes. TechVex CRM and ERP support multi-currency transactions including INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, and SGD.

## Pricing & Billing Questions

**Q: Can I add or remove users anytime?**
A: Yes. Users can be added anytime (billed prorated). Users can be removed at the next billing cycle.

**Q: Do you offer discounts for startups or NGOs?**
A: Yes. We offer a 20% startup discount for companies less than 3 years old with fewer than 50 employees. NGOs and educational institutions get 30% off.

**Q: What happens after the free trial?**
A: After the trial, you can choose a paid plan. Your data is preserved. If you don't upgrade within 30 days, your data is archived (recoverable for 90 days).
